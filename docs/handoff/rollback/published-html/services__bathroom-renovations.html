Bathroom Renovation in Toronto & GTA

Homewrap Construction provides complete bathroom renovation Toronto services, managing the full process from layout and design through material selection, permits, construction, and finishing. Our team plans every project around the details that matter behind the finishes, including waterproofing, ventilation, drainage, and proper installation, helping ensure your bathroom is built to perform as well as it looks.

Homewrap is a design-build contractor handling bathroom renovations across Toronto and the GTA. One team runs the full process, from layout and design through material selection, permits where required, and every trade, start to finish.

A bathroom is the most unforgiving room to build. Behind the tile sits waterproofing, ventilation, and drainage slope, and any one of them done poorly shows up years later as mold, rot, or a floor that never quite drains. We plan for what you won't see, because that's what decides whether the room holds up long after the finishes stop impressing you

Bathroom Renovation Services

Homewrap provides complete bathroom renovation Toronto services, managing each project from early design and layout planning through construction and final finishing. Whether the goal is a full transformation, a more functional layout, or a custom shower redesign, every decision is considered in relation to how the room should look, feel, and perform over time.

A refined Toronto bathroom renovation depends just as much on the work behind the finishes as the visible design. Plumbing, drainage, waterproofing, ventilation, lighting, tile preparation, fixtures, and material selections are coordinated from the outset as one integrated build. Homewrap also manages layout changes, heated flooring, custom showers, permits, and inspections where required, creating a bathroom that feels polished, durable, and carefully resolved.

 
Ensuite Bathroom Renovations

Homewrap approaches every primary bathroom renovation in Toronto with the level of planning and refinement the space deserves. Each ensuite begins with a carefully considered layout, positioning the tub, shower, vanity, storage, and circulation around the way the homeowners actually use the room and its connection to the primary bedroom. Features such as double vanities, heated floors, custom storage, and premium finishes can then be integrated to create a more elevated, comfortable retreat.

 
Main & Family Bathroom Renovations

Homewrap approaches family bathroom renovations in Toronto with an emphasis on refined functionality and everyday comfort. The layout is carefully considered around how the household moves through the space, with fixture placement, integrated storage, layered lighting, and durable finishes selected to support daily use. Whether the design calls for a tub, walk-in shower, or both, every element is tailored to create a polished, practical bathroom that feels elevated without sacrificing ease of use.

 
Small Bathroom Renovations

For compact bathroom renovations in Toronto, Homewrap focuses on making every part of the existing footprint work harder. Fixture sizing, layout, storage, lighting, and finishes are carefully coordinated to create a polished full bathroom with a tub, shower, or combination. The result is a refined, highly functional space that feels considered rather than constrained by its size.

 
Powder Room Renovations

For powder room renovations in Toronto, Homewrap treats the smaller footprint as an opportunity for more deliberate design. A custom vanity, feature tile, refined lighting, statement fixtures, and carefully selected finishes can give the room a distinctive character, creating a polished guest-facing space that feels elevated, cohesive, and intentionally designed.

 
Walk-In Showers & Tub-to-Shower Conversions

Transforming a tub into a custom shower offers an opportunity to bring greater refinement and clarity to the room. Within a Toronto bathroom renovation, Homewrap carefully resolves waterproofing, drainage, tile composition, recessed niches, and glass detailing so the shower feels considered as a complete architectural element, with every finish aligned to the overall character of the space.

 
Basement Bathroom Additions

A basement bathroom requires a different level of technical planning than one located on an upper floor. As part of a Toronto bathroom renovation, Homewrap coordinates below-grade plumbing, slab cutting where required, ventilation, drainage, permits, and fixture placement alongside the final layout and finishes. Whether adding a new bathroom or refining an existing one, the work is planned specifically around the conditions of the basement.

Our Process

How We Manage Your Bathroom Renovation

Bathroom renovations require precision sequencing across multiple trades. The order of operations matters: rough-in before waterproofing, waterproofing before tile, tile before fixtures.

Step 1
 
Site Visit & Consultation

We assess your existing bathroom, discuss your goals, and walk through the options for layout, materials, and budget.

Step 2
 
Design & Material Selection

Our team prepares a layout and helps you choose tile, fixtures, vanities, and finishes. You see the full plan and approve it before any demolition begins.

Step 3
 
Permits (if required)

Not every bathroom renovation needs a permit. Once the work moves plumbing, touches electrical, or changes structure, one is required, and we handle the application and the review process from there.

Step 4
 
Construction

Demolition, plumbing and electrical rough-in, waterproofing, tile installation, fixture setting, and finishing. A dedicated project lead manages every trade and every inspection.

Step 5
 
Walkthrough & Handover

We review the finished bathroom with you in detail. Everything is inspected, cleaned, and backed by warranty coverage.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Toronto?
 

Costs range from $5,000 to $12,000 for a powder room, $12,000 to $25,000 for a standard bathroom, and $25,000 to $50,000+ for a master ensuite with premium finishes. We provide a detailed quote after a site visit.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?
 

Most standard bathroom renovations take 3 to 6 weeks. Powder rooms can be completed in 1 to 2 weeks. Larger ensuites with custom tile and layout changes may take 6 to 8 weeks.

Do I need a permit for a bathroom renovation?
 

Not every bathroom renovation requires a permit. If you're relocating plumbing, adding electrical, or making structural changes, a permit is required. We handle the full permit process when needed.

Can you renovate my bathroom as part of a larger home renovation?
 

Yes. Many of our bathroom renovations are part of full home, main floor, or basement renovation projects. Having one team manage the full scope means better coordination and a smoother build.

What areas do you serve?
 

Toronto (North York, Scarborough, Etobicoke, East York) and the broader GTA including Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Markham, Mississauga, Aurora, Newmarket, Oakville, and Pickering.
